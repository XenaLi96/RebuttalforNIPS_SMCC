We sincerely thank the reviewer for asking what research becomes possible
because sMMC is larger, cell aligned, and context rich. This helped us organize

\questionsep
\textbf{Q1.}\quad
\textit{How can the new data promote research progress?}

\textbf{A1 (A unified cell-level benchmark).}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Task:} local-cell and tissue-context histology crops predict
aligned-cell RNA; no expression is supplied at inference.
\item \textbf{Protocols:} spatial holdout (5\% buffer), complete-sample
transfer, verified patient/donor holdout, and panel-matched cross-platform
transfer.
\item \textbf{Metrics:} gene/cell correlations, F1, marker/HVG performance, and
Average/Worst/Gap. STBoost lifts spot predictors through hierarchical
cell/context inputs.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{A3 (Transfer and context).}
\begin{itemize}
\item Across 18 same-organ held-out targets, top-50-HVG Gene Pearson averages
\textbf{0.170} (range \textbf{0.016--0.372}); these are sample-held-out unless
donors are verified.
\item Across eight HD source\(\rightarrow\)target organ pairs, UNI2-h macro
Gene Pearson, Cell Pearson, and F1 are \textbf{0.0151}, \textbf{0.2036}, and
\textbf{0.0815}. This difficult result is important: sample, platform, panel,
and composition shifts remain open problems.
\item We will reserve \emph{patient-held-out} for verified donors and report
cross-platform results with matched panels and platform-separated summaries.
\item Context audits using patient-CV or leave-one-site-out show Average--Worst
balanced-accuracy gaps of \textbf{0.258--0.975} across single-cell and pathology
encoders. The ovarian AK/AD/AL shift changes F1 from \textbf{0.289 to 0.072},
but will be labeled sample/age-confounded rather than causal.
\end{itemize}

\questionsep
\textbf{Q2.}\quad
\textit{How does heterogeneity affect downstream analysis, and what does cell
alignment add?}

\textbf{A1 (Measured consequence of resolution).}\enspace
Across six native-Xenium samples, cell/8/16/55-\(\mu\)m targets give Gene
Pearson \textbf{0.365/0.365/0.363/0.330}. Yet 55-\(\mu\)m pseudo-spots mix cell
types in \textbf{55.5--66.4\%} of dense-tissue regions, affecting
\textbf{73.8--81.0\%} of cells, versus
\textbf{3.5--4.1\%/7.0--8.3\%} in sparse tissue.

Moving spatial transcriptomics from spots toward cells therefore changes what
can be studied, not merely the benchmark resolution. It preserves cell identity
and neighborhood heterogeneity for cell-type/state analysis and cell--cell
communication; links morphology, RNA, and response at the cell unit for
virtual-cell and perturbation models; and enables tracing how perturbations
propagate through interacting cells across spatial tissue. These questions are
poorly posed when heterogeneous cells are collapsed into one spot.

\textbf{A2 (Revisions enabled by the reviewer's suggestions).}\enspace We will:
\begin{itemize}
\item place the benchmark task, STBoost definition, split construction, and
25-category results in the main text;
\item separate spatial, sample-held-out, verified patient-held-out, and
cross-platform claims;
\item report organ-macro and platform-separated Average/Worst/Gap with support,
panel overlap, and metadata missingness;
\item label each field as measured, processed, generated, or model output and
avoid causal or representativeness claims unsupported by donors.
\end{itemize}

We again thank the reviewer: these suggestions make the paper clearer about
both the opportunities enabled by a unified cell-aligned resource and the
substantial heterogeneity that future methods must solve.
